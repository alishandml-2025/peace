United Nations SDG 16

Building Peace, Justice & Strong Institutions

Promoting peaceful and inclusive societies, providing access to justice for all, and building effective, accountable and inclusive institutions at all levels.

Our Mission

Why SDG 16 Matters Today

Peace, justice, and strong institutions are the foundations upon which all other sustainable development goals are built.

☠

Reduce Violence

Significantly reduce all forms of violence and related death rates everywhere. Address conflict, insecurity, weak institutions, and limited access to justice.

  • Participate in community mediation and dialogue programmes
  • Volunteer with organisations that prevent youth violence
  • Promote non-violent conflict resolution in schools and workplaces
⚖

Access to Justice

Promote the rule of law and ensure equal access to justice for all. Build inclusive and effective legal systems accessible to every member of society.

  • Support legal aid clinics and pro-bono advocacy
  • Educate peers about their fundamental legal rights
  • Advocate for transparent and accessible court proceedings
⚙

Accountable Institutions

Develop effective, accountable and transparent institutions at all levels. Ensure responsive, inclusive, participatory and representative decision-making.

  • Engage with local governance, student unions, and civic groups
  • Report corruption and demand institutional transparency
  • Support policies that protect human rights and democratic values
